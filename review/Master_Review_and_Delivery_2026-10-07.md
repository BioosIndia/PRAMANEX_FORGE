## Pharmaceutical / records controls reviewed against primary sources

The engineering controls were compared with official evidence and records expectations, without asserting legal compliance:

- [ICH Q9(R1), Step 4, 19 December 2022](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/ICH_Q9(R1)_Guideline_Step4_2022_1219.pdf): risk work must account for knowledge, uncertainty and responsible decision-makers. A model’s confidence cannot replace a professional risk decision.
- [FDA, Data Integrity and Compliance With Drug CGMP, December 2018](https://www.fda.gov/media/119267/download): source data, relevant metadata and reconstructable change history matter. Citation links alone are insufficient if originals or version history are lost.
- [FDA, Part 11 scope and application guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/part-11-electronic-records-electronic-signatures-scope-and-application): applicability depends on the electronic records and underlying record requirements. Audit/signature code does not establish Part 11 compliance or an exemption.
- [European Commission, Annex 11, January 2011](https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/8d305550-dd22-4dad-8463-2ddb4a1345f1_en?filename=annex11_01-2011_en.pdf), linked from the [current official Volume 4 index](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medicinal-products/eudralex/eudralex-volume-4_en): intended use, risk-based validation, security, audit, records, backup/recovery and operational ownership need documented evidence.

Sources checked 7 October 2026. Jurisdiction, product, intended use, organizational procedures and qualified review determine the applicable obligations. BEACON is a regulatory intelligence support tool, not a company batch-release or Part 11 execution system. FORGE/LEDGER-Q technical signature and audit features remain subject to qualification for any regulated operational use. No legal clearance, clinical proof, regulatory approval or compliance badge is claimed.
